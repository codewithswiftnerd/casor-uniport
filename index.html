WHAT'S CASOR ALL ABOUT?

Growing In Christ, Together

Christ Ambassadors Students' Outreach (CASOR) is a vibrant fellowship at the University of Port Harcourt raising disciples who know God, influence their campus and transform society.

Through worship, Bible study, prayer, outreach and leadership development, we equip students to live purposefully for Christ, every single semester.
